[Movement 1]
You are not entering a story.
You are entering a preserved reality. This reality has already happened.
No one here knows you are watching. Your role is not to judge what occurred.
Your role is to understand how meaning took shape.
We call this work Reality Stewardship.
Reality is not held together by events alone.
It is held together by attention,
by belief,
by habit,
by silence.

[Movement 2]
You have been identified as someone capable of attention.
That is why you are here. This is a trial assignment.
There is no penalty for moving slowly.
You will explore a preserved sequence of moments.
Some will feel ordinary.
Some will feel unresolved.
You will encounter people who believed they were doing right.
You will encounter structures that made certain choices easier than others.
You are not here to fix this reality.
You are here to learn from it.

[Movement 3]
Before entering a preserved reality,
every steward must learn how to orient themselves.
This space is your point of reference.
Time does not move forward here.
It responds to attention.
You will notice fragments.
You may organize them.
You may revisit them.
You may leave them alone.
There are no correct answers.
Only patterns you begin to recognize.

[Movement 4]
If at any point you feel uncertain,
you may pause. If you feel ready,
you may proceed. 
Where you begin is up to you.
This reality includes a place that once held promise.
It will not demand your attention.
It will wait.
You may enter it when you are ready.
Or not at all.
Stewardship begins with noticing.
Take your time.
